## Excluded candidates

- Springhall 2018 (PMC8409818), AWMF 2024 (PMC11444747), and ISUOG 2023 first-trimester guideline: no confirmed open reuse license. AWMF matched figures are linked only.
- Ushakov 2024 first-trimester neurosonography: accessible abstract/captions, original asset download blocked. Link only.
- ISUOG 2024 Figure 3: suspected coarctation, not a normal 3VV example.
- ISUOG 2024 Figure 6b: severe hydronephrosis, not a normal coronal kidney example.
- ISUOG 2024 Figures 7–9: low-lying placenta/PAS/vasa previa cases, not general normal placental or cervix measurement examples.
- Ruican Figures 4–6: histological reconstruction, not real ultrasound images.
- Late-pregnancy limb case reports found during search described skeletal anomalies and were not used as normal examples.
- Figures from studies spanning several trimesters were not assigned to trimester 3 without panel-specific age evidence.
